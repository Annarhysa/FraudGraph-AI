Verdict
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIRMED across all 4 windows, WITH THE CORRECTED GRAPH: Merchant-only
consistently outperforms Full Graph — Window 0: 0.0858 vs 0.0549 (1.6x);
Window 1: 0.2293 vs 0.1670 (1.4x); Window 2: 0.2491 vs 0.1824 (1.4x);

and it is not an artifact of the merchant_offset bug either — the same
qualitative ordering (Merchant-only > Full Graph > No Graph >= Card-only)
survives the fix in every window tested, though the margin is smaller and
more consistent (~1.4-1.6x) than the pre-fix numbers suggested (which ranged
2.7-3.3x due to the corrupted merchant edges).
