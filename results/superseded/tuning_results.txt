FraudGraph AI — GraphSAGE Tuning Sweep (does more capacity close the XGBoost gap?)
======================================================================================

Motivation: the 5-seed robustness sweep (robustness_results.txt) found XGBoost
beat default GraphSAGE (30 epochs, hidden_dim=32) in 5/5 seeds (PR-AUC 0.0277 vs 0.0172 +/- 0.0018), but GraphSAGE's
validation AP was still rising sharply at the epoch-30 cutoff in every seed —
suggesting under-training rather than a real ceiling. This sweep retrains
GraphSAGE with larger budgets across 1 seed(s) ([0]) to check.

CAVEAT: this is a single-seed run (scaled down after a multi-seed x multi-config
sweep proved too slow on this CPU-only machine — full-batch GraphSAGE over a
~700K-node graph is expensive per epoch). Treat this as a directional signal,
not a statistically robust result — the earlier robustness sweep showed +/-0.0018
std on PR-AUC across seeds even at the cheap 30-epoch default config.
