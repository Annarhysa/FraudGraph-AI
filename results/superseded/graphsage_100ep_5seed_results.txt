FraudGraph AI — GraphSAGE @ 100 epochs, 5-seed confirmation + graph ablation
==========================================================================================

Fixed config: hidden_dim=32, epochs=100, lr=0.01, seeds=[0, 1, 2, 3, 4]
Same temporal split, same node/txn features as every other experiment in this repo.
Purpose: the single-seed tuning.py run found GraphSAGE(100ep)=0.0501 PR-AUC,
beating XGBoost's 0.0277 — but that was one seed and explicitly flagged as
directional only. This repeats it across 5 seeds, and re-runs the graph-vs-
no-graph ablation at 100 epochs (the earlier ablation was only measured at 30).
