Table 3 — Edge-type decomposition (Window 0, 100 epochs, 5 seeds each)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Condition                    PR-AUC              Gain over No-Graph
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No Graph (baseline)          0.0151 +/- 0.0003    --
Card-only                    0.0113 +/- 0.0004    -0.0038  (slightly negative, within noise)
Merchant-only                0.0858 +/- 0.0098    +0.0707
Full Graph (homogeneous)     0.0549 +/- 0.0067    +0.0398
Relation-Aware (separate)    0.0575 +/- 0.0053    +0.0424
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sum of individual gains (card + merchant) = 0.0669; Full Graph's actual gain (0.0398) falls
short of that sum -- card and merchant relationships overlap/interfere rather than combine
additively. Relation-aware aggregation recovers only a small fraction of the gap to
Merchant-only alone (+0.0026 PR-AUC over homogeneous Full Graph, not statistically distinguishable
given the seed variance) -- the dilution hypothesis from the (pre-fix) Phase 3 write-up is NOT
supported once merchant edges are correct; why mixing card edges in hurts remains an open question.
