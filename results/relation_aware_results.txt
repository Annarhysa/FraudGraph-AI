============================================================================================
FraudGraph AI — Relation-Aware GNN (Phase 3): does separating edge types help?
============================================================================================

Hypothesis: homogeneous SAGEConv mean-aggregates card and merchant neighbors
together with shared weights, diluting the strong merchant signal with the
uninformative card signal. RelationAwareSAGE gives each edge type its own
SAGEConv (separate weights), summing the two representations per layer instead
of aggregating over a single mixed edge set.

Config: hidden_dim=32, epochs=100, lr=0.01, seeds=[0, 1, 2, 3, 4], Window 0.
